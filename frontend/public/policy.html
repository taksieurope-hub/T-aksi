1. Terms & Conditions

1.1 Nature of the Service

T'aksi is not a transportation carrier, taxi company, or employer of Drivers. We solely provide the dashboard that facilitates this connection.

1.2 Independent Contractors

All Drivers operating on the T'aksi platform are independent, self-employed contractors. They are not employees, agents, or representatives of T'aksi.

  • Drivers use their own vehicles, set their own schedules, and are solely responsible for their vehicle's maintenance, insurance, and compliance with local laws in Georgia.
  • T'aksi is not liable for the actions, omissions, negligence, or misconduct of any Driver or Rider.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, T'aksi shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits, lost data, personal injury, or property damage related to, in connection with, or otherwise resulting from any use of the services.

2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

2.1 General Refund Policy

All charges are non-refundable unless explicitly determined otherwise by T'aksi in its sole and absolute discretion.

2.2 In-App Credit Only

If T'aksi determines that a refund or compensation is warranted (e.g., due to a technical error or severe driver delay), the refund will be issued exclusively as T'aksi Credit to the user's in-app wallet.

  • Under no circumstances will refunds be returned to the user's bank account, credit card, or issued as cash.
  • T'aksi Credits have no cash value, are non-transferable, and can only be used for future rides on the T'aksi platform.

2.3 Dispute Resolution

Any disputes regarding fares, routes, or ride quality must be reported to T'aksi Support within twenty-four (24) hours of the completed trip.

3. Privacy Policy

3.1 Data We Collect

To operate the platform, T'aksi collects:

  • Location Data: We track precise background and foreground GPS location from both Drivers and Riders to facilitate matching, navigation, and safety tracking.
  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, profile photos, and device information.

3.2 Data Sharing

We share necessary information between Riders and Drivers (e.g., first name, pickup location, vehicle model) strictly to fulfill the ride request. We do not sell your personal data to third-party marketers.
